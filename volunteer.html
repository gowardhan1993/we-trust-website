Why volunteer

Support people with dignity and presence.

Volunteers strengthen community programmes through time, care, local participation, and practical support. Even a few hours can help outreach, events, education, and administrative work move forward.

Who can join

Anyone committed to meaningful service.

We welcome individuals who want to contribute through field work, event support, teaching, fundraising, office help, and other forms of responsible volunteering.

Ways to help

Areas where volunteers can contribute

Field work

Support community visits, local outreach, coordination, and on-ground programme activities.

Events

Help with awareness programmes, logistics, registrations, and participant support.

Teaching

Contribute to education, guidance, mentoring, and skill-sharing sessions.

Fundraising

Assist in donor outreach, campaign promotion, and support-building efforts.

Admin support

Help with records, communication, documentation, and coordination tasks.

Flexible roles

Share any other skills or availability that may support the trust’s work.
