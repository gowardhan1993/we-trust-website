About WE-TRUST

WE-TRUST supports women and communities through empowerment, livelihood support, social care, and trust-based local action.

Who we are

Simple, local, and people-focused.

WE-TRUST is a charitable trust that works for women’s welfare, opportunity, and community support. The trust’s work includes empowerment programmes, livelihood initiatives, and support for deserving women and families.

Location

Based in Ambikapuram, Trichy.

WE-TRUST is publicly listed in Ambikapuram, Trichy. Volunteers can connect with the trust, cash donations are accepted, and membership does not require payment.
